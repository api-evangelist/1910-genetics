# 1910 Genetics

> 1910 Genetics (now branded 1910) is an AI-native biotechnology company advancing small and large molecule drug discovery with a multimodal AI platform powered by high-throughput laboratory automation. Its ITO platform integrates computational, wet-lab proxy, and wet-lab ground-truth biological data to pursue "modality agnostic" drug discovery. As of this profile the company publishes no public API, SDK, or developer documentation surface; it offers co-engineering collaborations to pharma and technology partners.
